# Duck & Chicken AI Detector

A Flask website that deploys a custom YOLOv8 object-detection model trained to identify ducks and chickens.

## Features

- Upload PNG, JPG, JPEG, or WEBP images
- Detect ducks and chickens
 

## Limitations

- Accuracy depends on the training dataset.
- Blurry, dark, small, or hidden animals may be missed.
- The application supports images only.
- Free hosting may take time to wake up.
